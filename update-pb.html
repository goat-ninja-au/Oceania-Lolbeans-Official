How to update your PB

No comments, no forms. Just tap your time on the rankings.

  1. Open the Oceania rankings and find your name.
  2. Tap your time for the map you got a PB on.
  3. Type your new time (like 31.284) and press Enter.
  4. Your old time stays with a gold border. The new one is waiting for approval.
  5. Once an admin approves it, your time updates. If not, the note tells you why.
