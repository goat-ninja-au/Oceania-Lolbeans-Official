About this page

This page shows the Oceania LOLBeans rankings from the community spreadsheet. Players are ranked by their total time across all 27 maps. You can browse every map time, see the leaderboard for each map, compare two players and see how close each player is to the Oceania record (OR) and world record (WR).

Get ranked

To join the rankings, fill out the registration form. If you're already on the sheet and set a new PB, write your old and new time in the comment section of the spreadsheet.

Register for the rankings Open the spreadsheet

Oceania records

Every new Oceania record must be confirmed. If you think you've broken one, send a DM to phoenix001699 on Discord with video proof of your run.

How to read this page

  • Overall rank is decided by the total time of all maps.
  • A blue OR badge means the player holds that map's Oceania record. A green WR badge means they hold the world record.
  • Card view colours each time by its rank on that map, from fastest (green) to slowest (red).
  • Table view colours each time by how far it is from the Oceania record. Click a map name at the top of a column to sort by that map (fastest first), and click again to reverse.
  • If a map has no time entered, 60 seconds are added to the total for that map, and it shows as a dash.
  • On the By map tab, a time faster than the world record is marked ⚠ Unverified.
  • Click a player to see their records, their biggest gaps to the Oceania record and their best map rankings, and to try the What-if simulator.

Times are read from the spreadsheet each time the page loads.
