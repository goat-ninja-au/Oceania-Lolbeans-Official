About the rankings

This page shows the Oceania LOLBeans rankings straight from the community spreadsheet. Players are ranked by their total time across all 27 maps. You can browse every map time, see the fastest players on each map and compare two players side by side.

Get ranked

To join the rankings, fill out the registration form. If you already have a time on the sheet and set a new PB, put your old and new time in the comment section of the spreadsheet.

Oceania records

New Oceania records must be confirmed before they count. If you think you've broken one, message phoenix001699 on Discord with video proof of your run.

How to read this page

  • Overall rank is decided by the total time of all maps.
  • A blue OR badge means the player holds that map's Oceania record. A green WR badge means they hold the world record.
  • Card view colours each time by its rank on that map, from fastest (green) to slowest (red).
  • Table view colours each time by how far it is from the Oceania record. Click a map name at the top of a column to sort by it, and click again to reverse.
  • If a player has no time on a map, it counts as 60 seconds in their total and shows as a dash.
  • On the By map tab, a time faster than the world record is marked ⚠ Unverified.

Times are read from the spreadsheet each time the page loads, so changes in the sheet show up here after a refresh.
