How the rankings work

Your ranking is based on your combined time across all 27 maps. Lower is better. If you haven't set a time on a map yet, it counts as 60 seconds until you do.

Career high is the best ranking a player has ever reached. It's checked against the spreadsheet every day, so a new best is saved automatically.
